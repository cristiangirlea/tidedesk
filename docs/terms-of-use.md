## 1. The connection service

TideDesk's connection service, rendezvous.tidedesk.app, is run by Cristian Girlea (the
operator). It lets a viewer find a host by its device ID and introduces the two
computers. The session itself always runs directly between them and never passes
through the service. TideDesk uses the service unless it is turned off in Settings,
where another service can also be named. What the service sees is described in the
privacy notes:
https://github.com/cristiangirlea/tidedesk/blob/main/docs/code-signing-policy.md#privacy

## 3. Acceptable use

- Do not use TideDesk to deceive anyone, for example by posing as technical support,

- Do not use TideDesk to access, watch or record anyone without their knowledge.

When you use the connection service:

- Do not disrupt, overload or probe it, or try to reach it in ways TideDesk does not.
- Do not look up device IDs in bulk or automatically, or ones you were not given.
- Do not use it to provide a service to others or to build another product.

If someone used TideDesk to reach your computer without your permission, or asked you
